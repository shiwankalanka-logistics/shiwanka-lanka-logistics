About SHIWANKA LANKA LOGISTICS

SHIWANKA LANKA LOGISTICS (PVT) LTD is a Sri Lankan manpower outsourcing and workforce solutions company established in 2020.


We provide reliable and flexible manpower solutions for businesses across logistics, courier, warehousing, manufacturing, retail and office operations.


Our workforce solutions include drivers, courier riders, warehouse staff, supervisors, office staff, factory workers, skilled and unskilled workers and other personnel based on our clients' requirements.


We focus on providing dependable employees while helping businesses manage their workforce efficiently and maintain smooth day-to-day operations.
